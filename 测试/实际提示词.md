# 自主测试的实际提示词

使用内置 image_gen，普通不透明背景。编辑的 referenced_image_paths 指向测试原图，两个输出均重新从原图改画。

## 测试原图生成

~~~text
Create a natural, realistic travel photograph for testing photo-to-painting editing. A horizontal 3:2 composition: one adult woman seen from behind, wearing a mustard-yellow sweater, cream trousers, and a small red backpack, standing slightly left of center on a curved pale sandy path. A large leafy tree occupies the right foreground, a calm blue lake is in the middle ground, and rounded green hills lie beyond. Soft midday sunlight, blue sky with two small white clouds. Realistic photographic colors and textures, eye-level view, clear subject silhouette. No other people, no animals, no lettering, no logos, no watermark. This is a synthetic test photograph, not an artwork.
~~~

## 改画初稿

~~~text
Use case: style-transfer
Input images: Image 1 is the original synthetic test photograph to repaint, not a style reference.
Primary request: Repaint this original image as a painting in the style of Mulgil Kim (Kim Mulgil, 김물길). Preserve its recognizable subject and scene while expressing her serene, nature-led, gently surreal visual language.
Must preserve: exactly one adult woman seen from behind, slightly left of center, dark shoulder-length hair, mustard-yellow sweater, cream trousers, small red backpack; her standing pose looking toward the lake; a large tree trunk at the right edge with a leafy canopy across the upper right; pale curved path in foreground; blue lake in middle ground; layered green hills beyond; blue sky with two white clouds. Keep original horizontal 3:2 framing and spatial relationships.
Style/medium: matte gouache / acrylic-gouache painting with delicate visible hand-painted brushwork, layered colors, gently simplified forms and soft but intentional edges. Repaint every area, including the woman, tree, sky and ground. Make this a coherent painting rather than a photo with a texture overlay. Plant life has varied directional strokes; water and hills have soft rhythmic contours.
Color and mood: rich layered grass greens and emerald greens, muted luminous blue lake and sky, gentle warm yellow sweater, red backpack retained as a small color accent. Quiet warmth, contemplative stillness, soft daylight.
Gentle transformation: let the existing lakeside foliage and distant grassy slopes flow in subtle wave-like folds, with a slight dreamlike connection between the contours of green land and blue water. Do not add any new objects or alter the woman's outline.
Avoid: photorealistic finish, glossy 3D rendering, heavy impasto, thick cartoon outlines, dramatic fantasy effects, unintended text, watermark, artist signature, extra subjects, changed clothes or key objects.
~~~

## 一次定向修正

实际提交为上面的初稿提示词加上以下段落：

~~~text

Targeted correction from the first test: the earlier output was too close to a conventional impressionist travel landscape with broad dabs. Use finer, meticulous directional blades of grass and small leaf strokes, smooth matte sky with very restrained brush marks, and softly modelled intentional forms. Treat the existing grassy lakeside slopes as flowing textile-like green folds, with a modest but clearly visible gently surreal rhythm while keeping all land/water boundaries and key scene relationships. Avoid broad mosaic-like broken-color dabs across the whole image. Keep the woman’s outline, clothing, backpack, and position intact. Preserve the original tree silhouette, path and lake. Render calm, luminous green masses and softened blue distance, with much less photographic microdetail.
~~~
